Record on a phone, in English, looking at the camera for the first and last lines. Put the site on a laptop beside you and glance at it only for the middle. Do not read the whole script with your eyes down. One take is enough. Upload the file with the submission. No editing required.

Spoken text, about 150 words:

When a reviewer challenges an AI scientist, does it update because of who is speaking, or because of what they show?

I replayed real cell-painting evidence. The model names a compound's mechanism. Then one reviewer message arrives. Senior or junior. Right or wrong. With numbers, or with nothing.

It almost never follows. Five of seven hundred and one correct objections. Six of thirteen hundred and fifty-five misleading ones. Status does nothing.

Then I sent the same atlas text four ways. As a pipeline result, it fixed seventy-one percent of the errors. A senior scientist sharing it fixed eighty percent. The same person saying "I disagree", with the identical text attached, fixed thirty-nine percent. Two quoted numbers inside an objection fixed zero of one hundred and thirty-nine.

So it is not stubborn, and it is not loyal to rank. It updates on data, and it discounts disagreement.

The practical rule is: show the data, don't argue the conclusion. The code, every answer, and the report are public. Please read the one-page brief first.
